## Focus Areas

### AI Product Quality

Reviewing AI-powered product experiences, including AI assistants, RAG products, agent workflows, recommendation flows, and LLM-based user interactions.

Key questions:

* Is the AI behavior useful, reliable, and understandable?
* Can users trust the output?
* Are failure cases handled clearly?
* Is there enough explainability for important decisions?

---

### FinTech QA & Trust-Critical Flows

Reviewing financial product flows, dashboards, scoring systems, onboarding experiences, and data-driven user journeys.

Key questions:

* Does the product explain important numbers and scores?
* Are financial metrics clear and traceable?
* Are edge cases and empty states handled properly?
* Does the experience build user trust?

---

### Release Readiness

Assessing whether an MVP or early-stage product is ready for broader testing, pilot users, or production rollout.

Key questions:

* Are core flows covered by smoke tests?
* Are high-risk workflows identified?
* Are onboarding, error states, and recovery paths tested?
* Is there a clear QA priority before launch?

---

### AI System Reliability Review

Reviewing AI and automation systems from a reliability and governance perspective.

Key questions:

* Where can the AI system fail?
* What should be verified before output reaches users?
* What needs human review?
* What should be logged, measured, or monitored?
